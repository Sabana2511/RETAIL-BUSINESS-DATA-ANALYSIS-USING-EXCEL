# 🛍️ Retail Business Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Retail Business Analytics Dashboard** built in **Microsoft Excel** to 
analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, inventory status, and regional revenue trends. 
The dashboard transforms raw retail data into actionable insights that support business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Monitor overall business performance using key KPIs.
* Analyze customer membership and revenue contribution.
* Identify top-performing and low-performing products.
* Track inventory levels and stock availability.
* Evaluate revenue performance across regions and categories.
* Visualize monthly sales trends for better forecasting.

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning Process

* Handled missing values by filling blanks with appropriate values or labels.
* Removed duplicate records to improve data quality.
* Standardized text formats and corrected inconsistencies.
* Validated data types for dates, revenue, quantities, and IDs.
* Checked and resolved formula errors before analysis.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Platinum members contribute the highest share of revenue.
* Female customers generate 57% of total revenue.
* North region records the highest revenue, while West region records the lowest.
* Sports and Electronics categories are the strongest revenue contributors.
* Portable Power Bank is the top revenue-generating product.
* Nearly half of the products are in low-stock condition, indicating replenishment needs.
* Sales peaked in March and remained relatively stable during the second half of the year.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Replenish low-stock and out-of-stock products promptly.
* Strengthen customer loyalty programs to increase high-value memberships.
* Focus marketing efforts on underperforming regions.
* Promote high-performing products and review strategies for low-performing products.
* Use sales trends to improve demand forecasting and inventory planning.
